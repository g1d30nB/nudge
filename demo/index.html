Built for the meeting you already have

Most teams do not need another place to track work. They need the Monday conversation to end with something written down that everyone agrees on, and the Friday conversation to start from it.

A plan is a short list of outcomes for the week, each with an owner and a reason. Items carry over automatically when they are not done, and the carry-over is visible, so a task that has slipped three weeks running is hard to ignore.

On Friday each owner writes two sentences: what happened, and what they would do differently. Those notes become next week's starting point rather than a report nobody opens.
